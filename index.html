About the company

A Dubai delivery company built around reliability

SOUMT ALMADINA DELIVERY SERVICES L.L.C S.O.C is a Dubai-based order delivery company operating from Al Khabeesi, Dubai. We carry orders from the point of pickup to the customer's door, so the businesses we work with can stay focused on what they sell.

We are licensed for delivery services by the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism. That means every order is handled by one accountable team, with the same standard applied from the first pickup to the last handover.

We take on deliveries for restaurants, shops, online sellers and companies, from a single order to regular daily runs.
